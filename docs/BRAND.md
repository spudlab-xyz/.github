# Brand guidance

Spudlab's identity should be clear, restrained and accessible. Branding must not reduce readability in GitHub's light or dark themes, and it must not attempt to override GitHub's interface styling.

## Palette

| Colour | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charcoal | `#373b3e` | Primary dark background |
| Cool grey | `#bec8d1` | Text and neutral details |
| Mint | `#86cecb` | Primary brand accent and logo |
| Deep teal | `#137a7f` | Supporting colour |
| Magenta | `#e12885` | Selective emphasis |

Use sufficient contrast for text, controls and diagrams. The full palette documents the available identity; an asset does not need to use every colour.

## Approved logo

The approved logo is a solid mint laboratory flask on charcoal, with a potato-shaped negative-space cutout in the lower chamber and three small mint skin marks.

The approved source image is not currently stored in this repository. When supplied, add the unchanged GitHub-ready asset as `spudlab-github-avatar.png`, keep it below 1 MB and use meaningful alt text such as “Spudlab flask logo” wherever it appears. Do not redraw, approximate or substitute the logo.

## Use

- Prefer simple layouts and avoid decorative repetition.
- Check text and essential graphics for contrast in both light and dark contexts.
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.
- Keep clear space around the logo and avoid stretching, recolouring or adding effects.
- Do not use the branding to imply sponsorship, partnership or endorsement.

This is Spudlab branding. Similarities in colour to other identities do not imply affiliation with Hatsune Miku or any rights holder.
