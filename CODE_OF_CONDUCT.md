# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Spudlab welcoming and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and good-faith mistakes.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect privacy and stop unwelcome interactions.
- Disclose conflicts and material agent involvement.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants when needed to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid conflicts of interest.

For non-sensitive concerns, open an issue in the relevant repository and include **Conduct concern** in the title. That issue is public. Spudlab does not currently have a verified private conduct-reporting route, so do not disclose sensitive conduct details publicly. This limitation remains until a human-controlled private route is enabled and tested.

For immediate self-protection, use GitHub's blocking tools. You may also [report abuse to GitHub](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) when the content or account is on GitHub. Those are GitHub routes, not confidential reports to Spudlab and not substitutes for a Spudlab enforcement decision.
